An AI agent plugged into open geospatial tools. Weather, routes, places, air quality and satellite images today. Ships and planes next. Type a question, watch the answer land on the map.

ASK • MAP • TRACK
ready
Pick a question or write your own.

Live answers from open data. 10 questions per visitor per day. Questions are sent to a third-party AI model, so don't type personal information. AI answers can be wrong: check anything important.

Three steps, no map skills.

1

You ask

Plain language. "Weather in Nuuk?" or "Route from Oslo to Stockholm."

2

The agent picks tools

It calls open tools for geocoding, weather, air quality and routing.

3

You see it

The camera flies to the answer and the data source is shown next to it.

Everything it can reach.
